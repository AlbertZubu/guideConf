Full Days Workshops Visits

In Your City

Full days organised in the city where participants live or are staying — three formats.

Taste & Learn

A full day exploring one theme, alternating theoretical and participatory talks with outdoor visits and hands-on experiences, punctuated by two tasting moments — a relaxed, enjoyable alternative to the sometimes overly academic feel of traditional activities.

Trek

An urban hike built around walking and discovery. The day has a light sporting dimension while staying accessible, grounded in gentle physical activity. The route is punctuated with breaks, tastings and visits — but walking stays the thread running through the whole experience.

Lab

An intensive day built around deep learning or hands-on project work, usually run in one place with little travel involved. Participants might, for example:

  • Build a website in a day
  • Create an Android app
  • Discover and practise photography
  • Take part in a painting intensive
  • Explore another creative or technical practice

By the end of the day, participants have gained a skill, tried a new practice, or produced something concrete.
