# Out of Town destination images

Thumbnails for the day-trip destinations listed under Full Days →
Out of Town (catalogue/services-full-days.html). Rendered in a 4:3 tile
with `object-fit: cover`.

Giverny and Auvers-sur-Oise are copied from assets/images/excursions/wiki/;
the other six were pulled from Wikipedia and resized to 800 px max,
82 % quality. All are placeholders pending final photography.
